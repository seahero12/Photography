Self Portrait + Artist Statement

Finding the frame before taking the shot.

I work with digital photography using both smartphone cameras and professional DSLR cameras. My focus is mainly on minute details, whether they appear within large landscapes or smaller subjects. My photography shares how I see details, color palettes, textures, and shapes in my head, along with my perspective on what feels visually interesting or unusual.

When taking photos, I try to find colors that work together throughout the frame, and I usually edit with one or two main colors in mind. Most of the time, I lean toward finding contrasting colors.

Favorite Image

Lighthouse

This is my favorite picture from the year because the lighthouse feels calm, structured, and visually unusual. I like how the frame uses soft light and muted color to make the shape of the building stand out without making the image feel too busy.

Three Key Concepts

What stayed with me.

Composition Creates Direction

Composition is more than placing a subject in the middle of the frame. Leading lines, framing, symmetry, and rule of thirds all change the way a viewer travels through an image. I learned that a photo becomes stronger when every edge and empty space has a reason to be there.

Light Changes the Mood

Light can make the same subject feel dramatic, soft, mysterious, or playful. Directional light creates shape and contrast, while softer light can make details feel more natural. This class helped me look for light first, because the light often decides what kind of photograph the scene can become.

Editing Supports the Story

Editing should not just make a photo look more intense; it should support the idea behind the image. Cropping, contrast, color correction, and compositing can guide attention when they are used with control. I learned that the best edits feel purposeful, not like effects added at the end.

17 Gallery Sections

Project archive.

01

Falloween

This project used seasonal imagery to create a playful fall atmosphere with a slightly strange edge. The edited images focus on color, subject placement, and mood so the set feels connected.

02

Ghost in Class Photoshoot

The ghost shoot explored how editing can change an ordinary classroom scene into something surreal. Showing the edited and unedited versions together makes the creative choices easier to compare.

03

Homework Composition

This collection practices the compositional tools that make a photograph easier to read. The images include leading lines, framing, rule of thirds, and symmetry.

04

Sonder Photo Essay

The Sonder essay is about noticing that every person has a life as complicated as your own. The sequence uses repeated human moments and quiet framing to create a connected photo story.

05

Multiplicity

The multiplicity composite combines repeated versions of the subject into one believable scene. The challenge was making the edits feel consistent through lighting, scale, and placement.

06

Light and Shadow

This shoot focused on contrast, highlights, and the way shadows define form. The edits use directional light to make the subjects feel more dramatic and dimensional.

07

Group Still Life

The group still life project required careful arrangement of objects, light, and background. The final images use proximity and repeated forms to make the setup feel intentional.

08

Instagram Ad

The Instagram ad project treated photography as visual communication with a clear audience. The still images and GIF work together to make a product-style idea feel polished and shareable.

09

In Class Stop Motion GIF

This project used a sequence of still images to create motion. The GIF shows how small changes between frames can create rhythm, pacing, and a simple visual narrative.

10

Golden Ratio Food

The golden ratio food shoot explored mathematical composition through food styling. The images use spiral, triangle, and phi-based arrangements to create balance and movement.

11

Family Recipe Food Blog

The food blog project combined photography with layout and storytelling. The pages present the recipe clearly while the favorite food image gives the project a stronger visual anchor.

12

5 Poses

This assignment studied posing and presentation through a slide deck and a sixth finished image. The collection shows how body position, framing, and expression can change the feeling of a portrait.

13

Treasure Hunts 1-9

The treasure hunts pushed me to search for specific visual ideas instead of waiting for them to appear. Together, the nine images show practice with depth, color, rhythm, diffusion, and other compositional prompts.

14

Photoshop Journals

The Photoshop journals documented editing experiments and finished digital work. They show growth in using tools intentionally while keeping track of process and choices.

15

Hands Portraits

The hands portraits focus on expression without relying on a face. Gesture, texture, and light become the main tools for showing personality and mood.

The Photoshop with Younger Self assignment was excused.

Portrait This or That

This project used a triptych format to express the identity of the subject posing. In this project, the portraits were meant to give a glimpse into the workings of my mom's daily life.
