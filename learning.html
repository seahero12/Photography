Three Key Concepts

What stayed with me.

Composition Creates Direction

Composition is more than placing a subject in the middle of the frame. Leading lines, framing, symmetry, and rule of thirds all change the way a viewer travels through an image. I learned that a photo becomes stronger when every edge and empty space has a reason to be there.

Light Changes the Mood

Light can make the same subject feel dramatic, soft, mysterious, or playful. Directional light creates shape and contrast, while softer light can make details feel more natural. This class helped me look for light first, because the light often decides what kind of photograph the scene can become.

Editing Supports the Story

Editing should not just make a photo look more intense; it should support the idea behind the image. Cropping, contrast, color correction, and compositing can guide attention when they are used with control. I learned that the best edits feel purposeful, not like effects added at the end.
